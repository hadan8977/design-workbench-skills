# Yu Apple deck workflow

Scale work to the request; a one-page revision does not require a new deck.

## Narrative and copy

Identify audience, occasion, intended decision, required facts, formats, and user wording. Separate verified facts, judgments, proposals, visions, and unresolved information. Client pitches emphasize concrete benefits and action. Personal talks can preserve the author's humor, hesitation, and natural language mixing when present in the source; do not invent a persona.

Turn topic labels into claims, findings, meaningful questions, or actions. Support them with evidence and causal explanation. Preserve quotations and exact copy instructions. Avoid repetitive slogans or abstract promises that hide who does what and why it matters.

For a full deck, map each page's claim, evidence or scene, role, connection to adjacent pages, composition, and information status. Lead from the audience's current understanding to a concrete next action. Do not mechanically split document headings into slides.

State shared premises once, outside parallel modules. Then consider diagrams, process lines, imagery, or splitting a page before reducing type size. Titles usually occupy one or two semantic lines, broken at natural phrases. Supporting blocks follow content needs. A footer should add meaning rather than repeat the title.

Marketing concepts explain both why people would pay attention and how that attention connects to the business or next action. A logo alone does not establish the connection. Label proposed topics and targets; never invent real rankings, views, media coverage, or deployment progress.

## Choose a page role

| Content need | Useful structure | Check |
| --- | --- | --- |
| Memorable claim | Large statement on light or dark with one emphasis | Supporting copy preserves the pause |
| Meaning of a number | Number, unit, scope, finding, source | Timeframe, denominator, and comparability are clear |
| New concept | Relationship diagram or concrete scene | Decoration does not substitute for explanation |
| Process | Connected path, nodes, direction | Stage count follows reality; future stages are labeled |
| Parallel capabilities | Shared container, consistent icons, one shared premise | No duplicated conditions or nested containers |
| Alternatives | Aligned contrast or mirrored views | Comparable scale, visible area, padding, and baselines |
| Real case | Main image, annotations, clear claim | Crop retains evidence and essential action |
| Campaign concept | Proposed scene, attention mechanism, brand connection | No fabricated proof of success |
| Budget or configuration | Table, total, scope, necessary notes | No double counting or decorative interruptions |
| Transition or closing | Sparse statement, image, or next action | No irrelevant content added for variety |

## Build and revise

Read [visual language](visual-language.md), then establish stable fonts, colors, spacing, sizes, radii, and shadows. For HTML copy the bundled template into the project; leave the installed template unchanged. Keep assets in portable relative paths.

For a new full deck, calibrate a statement page, an image-led page, and a dense information page. If the user asked to approve samples, follow that cadence. If they authorized the whole deck, calibrate internally and continue. Reuse already approved samples without another approval step.

Inspect overview pacing, repeated silhouettes, and density, then each page's line breaks, alignment, sources, image quality, factual status, and readability. Consistent layouts can aid comparison. Apply precise copy changes narrowly. Convert feedback into reusable judgments without making project-specific clients, budgets, jokes, or page counts universal requirements.

## Export acceptance

Read [tooling](tooling.md) for requested formats. Check page identifiers, counts, missing images, console errors, overflow, and source labels. Re-render affected pages and refresh dependent overviews and exports together; partial previews must not mix versions.

4K PNG pages must be rendered at 3840×2160. Inspect PDF independently of HTML screenshots for page count, 16:9 ratio, blanks, cropping, navigation residue, font substitution, gradients, and transparency. Preserve native text and vectors where feasible. Automatic checks support visual inspection; they do not establish fidelity or factual accuracy.
